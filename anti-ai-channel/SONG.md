# "Made By No One" (channel theme song, for Suno or another AI music tool)

The song fits the concept: it's sung by an AI, and it knows it. It sounds like a heartfelt human ballad until the lyrics give it away.

## Style prompt (paste into the "Style of Music" box)

```
cinematic indie ballad, solo felt piano intro, intimate breathy vocal, heartbeat kick drum enters later, swelling strings, emotional anthemic build, sudden silence before final line, 72 bpm, A minor
```

Suno doesn't accept artist names in prompts, so describe the sound instead (as above).

## Fitting it to the video

- Aim for about 2:15 to 2:20. If Suno returns a longer track, fade it out at 2:18 in your editor.
- Line up the **[Break] silence** with **"Except no human made this"** (about 1:26). That's the reveal moment.
- The rendered video already has an ambient drone and a flatline tone. Lower it under the song, or mute it.

## Rights and licensing

- On Suno's **free plan**, songs are for non-commercial use only. To monetise the channel, generate the track on a **paid plan** (check Suno's current terms; they change).
- The major record labels have sued Suno over its training data. That's worth covering in a video. It's exactly your channel's subject.
